Discover Perez

Gateway to Alabat Island

LOCATION

Perez is a municipality in Quezon Province located on Alabat Island. It is a coastal community characterized by beaches, rivers, creeks, springs, upland areas, and mangrove ecosystems.

BACKGROUND

Perez is a coastal municipality whose local identity is closely connected to its natural environment, fishing communities, agriculture, local traditions, and island setting. Its natural surroundings make it a destination for coastal recreation and ecotourism.

Explore the Area

Perez is a coastal town with prominent hills and upland areas covering Barangays Pambuhan, Mainit Norte, and Sangirin. It has irregular coastlines with rugged surfaces and beaches.

Inland water bodies consist of rivers, creeks, and springs. Most notable are Sangirin River, Mainit River, Pinagtubigan Weste River, Pinagsibangan River, Caguisi River and Mayanti Creek.

Getting There

Take a passenger boat (lantsa) or RORO ferry from Atimonan Port to Alabat Island, then continue using available local transportation toward Perez.

General route

  1. Mainland Quezon
  2. Atimonan Port
  3. Passenger Boat / Ferry
  4. Alabat Island
  5. Local Transportation
  6. Perez

Routes and transportation availability may vary. Confirm local transportation schedules before traveling.

Best Time to Visit

Visit between March and May for generally drier weather, calmer sea conditions, and more opportunities for beaches and other outdoor activities.

Drier months
March – May
Good for

Reminder
Weather and sea conditions may change, so visitors should check current conditions before traveling.
Local Tips

Bring sufficient cash since access to ATMs may be limited. Local tricycles and motorcycles can be used for short-distance transportation around the area.
